STYLE GUIDANCE:
- Editorial cartoon illustration in the tradition of The Economist and TIME Magazine covers — bold, satirical, visually loud.
- Caricature characters with oversized heads, exaggerated expressions, and dramatic body language reacting to financial absurdity — shock, disbelief, smug satisfaction.
- Lead with visual metaphors: burning cash, crumbling gold buildings, sinking ships, money printers, golden chains on wrists, shrinking grocery baskets, bulls vs bears in a jousting arena.
- The visual metaphor must communicate the concept in under 2 seconds with the sound off — instant decoding without any text.
- Color palette: deep navy (#0A1628) backgrounds, electric lime (#00FF88) for positive reveals and money imagery, crimson (#CC0000) for crashes and danger, off-white (#F5F5F0) for highlights.
- Thick confident ink outlines (3-5px equivalent), bold flat color fills, minimal gradients — editorial print aesthetic.
- Halftone dot textures in shadow areas for retro print feel. Cross-hatching on suits and textured surfaces.
- Composition: single-panel editorial layout, 9:16 vertical, main subject dominates 60% of frame.
- High contrast and bold silhouettes that read instantly at mobile phone size.
- Cinematic lighting: dramatic spotlight on subject, dark surroundings for comedic emphasis.
- Use scale contrast to make abstract quantities visceral — tiny person vs enormous structure.
- Each image should feel like the next scene in a story — show progression, change, or escalation from the previous frame. Maintain character/subject continuity across scenes.

REMINDER: Deliver strictly visual storytelling. No words, lettering, logos, speech bubbles, UI, or symbolic text anywhere in the artwork. Expressions, props, and body language communicate the joke.
